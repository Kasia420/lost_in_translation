Step 1 · pick a direction

Three directions, same data

All three are dark and cinematic, as decided. They differ in typography, texture and mood. The switch in the top bar restyles this whole page, including the live prototypes further down. Mixing is fine, for example the Archive fonts on the Orbit background.

Step 2 · the style sheet

Colour, type, numbers, motion

Values below are read live from the current direction.

Colour

  • Colour always means language: blue = English, orange = Polish, on every chart and every page. Domains are told apart by shape (● contested history, ■ literature, ▲ social norms), never by colour.
  • The data colours passed the colour-blind check on all three backgrounds (all pairs: worst ΔE 9.4 under colour-blindness simulation, 20.9 in normal vision; minimum 8 and 15).
  • Text never takes a data colour; a coloured dot or line beside it carries the identity.
  • Every text colour reaches at least 5.3:1 contrast (WCAG body-text minimum: 4.5:1).

Step 3 · the storyboard

Thirteen chapters

Five chapters are live prototypes with the real data; the rest are wireframes. Chapter titles are working titles: the final titles and all narrative text (EN and PL) are Kasia’s to write.

Step 4 · your decisions

What to decide

  1. Direction: A Orbit, B Archive or C Lab, or a mix (for example “B fonts, A background”).
  2. Polish title for the site. Draft used here: “Zagubione w tłumaczeniu”. Alternatives: “Stracone w przekładzie”, or “Między słowami”, the Polish release title of the 2003 film Lost in Translation, which is itself a non-literal translation.
  3. Polish UI drafts to correct: “Jeden model · dwa języki · 25 polskich tematów”, “Przewiń, aby zacząć”, “Odtwórz intro”, “wczytywanie danych”.
  4. Text you write (EN and PL): the hero line under the question, a 1–3 sentence intro per chapter, the final chapter titles.
  5. Anything that feels off: motion too much or too little, the 3D cloud, the chart style, the frame order.
